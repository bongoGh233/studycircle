# Brand assets

## UENR crest

- **Files:** `uenr-crest.png` (header lockup), plus `favicon-32.png`,
  `favicon-64.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png` and `icon-512.png` (white rounded
  plate with the same crest).
- **Source:** University of Energy and Natural Resources website media library,
  <https://uenr.edu.gh/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/UENR-LOGO-spline-Converted-1.png>.
- **Retrieved:** 26 October 2026.
- **Changes:** the crest was trimmed to its transparent content bounds
  (429 × 557 px). The icon files place that crest on a white rounded square so
  the white details stay visible on light and dark browser chrome. No colours or
  shapes were redrawn.

The UENR name and crest are the property of the University of Energy and Natural
Resources. They are used here only to identify the university this student
project is built for. UENR does not operate, sponsor, endorse or support UENR
StudyCircle. This is an independent student project.

If you fork or reuse this project, replace these files with your own artwork
unless you have permission to use the UENR marks.
